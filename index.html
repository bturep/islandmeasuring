Services

As-built drawings

Renovators, designers, cabinet and flooring trades

A dimensioned plan of the building as it stands today: every wall with its thickness, every door and window located. Renovators quote from it, cabinet and flooring trades fabricate from it, and designers use it as their base file, including for secondary and garden suites. Ceiling heights, sill heights and window sizes are added when you ask for them.

Commercial measure

Landlords, tenants, property managers

The drawings on file for most commercial buildings date from construction, and the space has usually been divided and re-divided since. We measure what is there now and give you a current plan, with the floor area of each unit or bay worked out and a note on the drawing saying where it was measured to. If the space is occupied, we come before opening or after closing.

Large, care and institutional buildings

Care homes, seniors residences, multi-unit residential

These are quoted per building. If plans already exist on file, we can digitize them and check the dimensions on site, which usually costs less than measuring a big building from scratch. Either way, nothing is delivered that hasn't been checked on site.

Listing plan

Realtors and sellers

A measured floor plan of every level, with the square footage of each, in your inbox within 48 hours.

Every job comes as a PDF and an editable CAD file (DWG). We can also add interior elevations, a reflected ceiling plan, a photo record of every room, photos locating the electrical, plumbing and mechanical, a site measure, or field measures and templating for trades. A 24-hour rush is available. Each is priced in your quote, so there are no extras afterwards.

How it works

  1. Email us the address, what the drawing is for, and a rough size. You'll have a fixed price back the same day.
  2. We visit once. A typical house takes about two hours. Commercial space takes longer, and your quote says how long.
  3. The person who measured your building draws it. Nothing is handed off or sent out for drafting.
  4. You get the PDF and the CAD file. If something needs changing, one round of revisions is included.

Scope

We measure and draw what exists, and that is all we do. We don't design, give building-code or zoning advice, apply for permits, assess wiring, plumbing or structure, or do legal surveys.

That is deliberate. You get a drawing with no opinion in it, and the designer, engineer or surveyor you hire next gets a base file they can trust. If your project needs one of them, we'll tell you.

FAQ

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of the building and how divided up it is, so every job is quoted individually. Send the address and what you need, and a fixed price comes back the same day. There is no hourly meter. The minimum booking is $500.

What do I get, and when?

A PDF and an editable CAD file (DWG). Listing plans arrive within 48 hours. As-builts and commercial jobs take longer, and your quote says exactly when. We keep every file, so if you need your plan again in five years, that's an email, not a re-measure.

Do you need access to every room?

Yes. A plan with a guessed room in it isn't worth much. We're used to working around tenants, lockboxes and open houses.

Is there a measurement standard?

For homes, yes. Floor area is reported to RMS / ANSI Z765, with detached houses measured at the exterior foundation and condos paint-to-paint. For commercial space it depends on the lease, so we calculate each unit's area from the measured plan and write on the drawing where it was measured to.

What if I need to cancel?

There's no charge with a day's notice. Inside 24 hours there is a $150 fee, because the day has usually been set aside for you.
