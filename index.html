How it works

  1. Email the address, what the drawing is for, and a rough size. A fixed quote comes back the same day.
  2. One site visit. A typical home takes about two hours; commercial space is measured around tenants and business hours.
  3. The plan is drafted in CAD by the same person who measured it.
  4. You receive the PDF and DWG. One revision round is included.

Scope

Measurement only. We document what exists: walls, openings, stairs, built-ins, room dimensions and floor area.

We do not provide design, building-code or zoning advice, permit applications, electrical, plumbing or structural assessment, or legal surveys. When a project needs a designer, an engineer, an architect or a BC Land Surveyor, we say so and hand over a clean file.

FAQ

What does it cost?

Every job is quoted individually. Send the address and what you need, and a flat quote comes back the same day. Minimum booking $500. Cancelling is free with a day's notice; inside 24 hours there is a $150 fee.

What do I get, and when?

A PDF and an editable CAD file (DWG). Listing plans in 48 hours; as-builts and commercial work on the schedule stated in your quote. Files are archived, so a copy years later is an email, not a re-measure.

Do you need access to every room?

Yes. A floor plan with a guessed room in it isn't worth much. We work around tenants, lockboxes, and open houses.

Is there a measurement standard?

Yes. Residential area is reported to RMS / ANSI Z765: detached homes at the exterior foundation, condos paint-to-paint. Commercial floor areas are calculated from the measured plan, unit by unit. Every drawing states the standard or method used.
