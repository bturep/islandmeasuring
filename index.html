04

Scope

The scope is written down so there are no surprises. You get facts on paper; the professionals you hire next get a base file they can trust.

Included in every measure

  • Interior & partition walls, room dimensions
  • Closets, stairs, built-in footprints
  • Door & window openings, located
  • Garage footprint
  • Square footage to the applicable standard, within 2%
  • Detached homes measured at exterior foundation; condos paint-to-paint
  • Basements measured, reported separately from above-grade area

What we don't do

  • Design services of any kind — no proposed plans, no layouts
  • Building-code or zoning advice
  • Permit applications or approvals
  • Electrical, plumbing, HVAC or structural assessment
  • Legal surveys — that's a BC Land Surveyor
  • Furniture & movable fixtures in drawings
  • Areas with under 5 ft of ceiling height in the reported square footage
Operating rules — Minimum booking $500. One site visit per job. One revision round included; further revisions billed hourly. Re-measures (the space changed) are a new job. Every drawing carries the note: "Existing-conditions documentation prepared for the purpose stated; not a legal survey; not design; dimensions to be verified independently before construction or purchase decisions." When your project needs a designer, engineer, registered architect, or land surveyor, we'll say so and hand over a clean file.

07

Frequently asked questions

How long does the measure take?

A typical home is about two hours on site. Commercial takes longer depending on the size and layout — your quote will say how long.

Do you need access to every room?

Yes. A floor plan with a guessed room in it isn't worth much. We work around tenants, lockboxes, and open houses.

What do I get, and when?

A PDF and an editable CAD file (DWG). Measured plans in 48 hours; as-builts and commercial on the schedule stated in your quote.

What does it cost?

Every job is quoted individually — send the address and what you need, and a flat quote comes back the same day. No hourly meter.

Is there a measurement standard?

Yes. Residential area is reported to RMS / ANSI Z765, commercial to the applicable BOMA standard — and every drawing states which one was used.

Do you keep the files?

Yes, every job is archived. If you need your plan again in five years, that's an email, not a re-measure.

What if I need to cancel?

No charge with a day's notice. Less than 24 hours, a $150 cancellation fee.
